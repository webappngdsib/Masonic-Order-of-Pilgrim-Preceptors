Friday, 26 February 2027

          
The Masonic Order of Pilgrim Preceptors

          
The Consecration of the four new Conclaves and the official Inauguration of the new Region of The Masonic Order of Pilgrim Preceptors in Greece will take place. Bro. Aristidis Samios, Past Grand Registrar and Designated Regional Governor, will assume the office of Regional Governor. From the Masonic year 2026–2027, each Conclave will meet twice annually. The Annual Regional Conclave will form part of a joint Masonic Weekend with the Provincial Grand Court of The Masonic Order of Athelstan in Greece, on the last Saturday of February (28 February 2026 and 27 February 2027). In 2027 the Pilgrim Preceptors meeting will take place on Friday 26 February, followed by the Athelstan Provincial Grand Court on Saturday 27 February.
